## Mobile App Development (iOS/Android)

**Feature Description**
Native mobile apps for iOS and Android platforms.

**Requirements**

- React Native or Flutter mobile apps
- Push notifications for deal alerts
- Offline access to meal plans
- Barcode scanning for products
- Share shopping list functionality
- App Store / Play Store deployment

**Priority:** Low | **Effort:** Very Large
**Note:** This is a major undertaking and should be considered after core web features are stable.
